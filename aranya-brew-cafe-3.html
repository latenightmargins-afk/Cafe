Our story

A little slower.
A lot better.

Aranya Brew Café was born in Kolkata in 2021 with one simple idea: make a place where excellent coffee and everyday life can coexist without rushing.

From specialty coffee and fresh bakery favourites to quiet corners made for focused work, Aranya is designed for students, young professionals, couples and anyone who wants to stay awhile.
